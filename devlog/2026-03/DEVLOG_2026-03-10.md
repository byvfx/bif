## Learnings
- Inverse-transpose for normal transforms is critical for non-uniform scale — classic CG bug
- `ControlFlow::Poll` vs `Wait` in winit: Poll spins, Wait sleeps between events
- Light selection probability must be included in MIS PDF when sampling one of N lights
- `sort+dedup` is simpler than `HashSet` when you're already sorting
- `AnyChannels<FlatSamples>` + `AnyChannels::sort()` eliminates combinatorial SpecificChannels explosion
- Delegating `ray_color` → `ray_color_with_aovs` is trivial since AOV capture is a few field assignments on first hit
