## Learnings

- A "metadata query" that constructs a domain object can carry hidden side effects — here global name counters. Pure lookups want pure tables.
- A consistency test between two paths that read the same underlying data is a tautology. Assert hand-written expectations instead.
- Reviewer subagents earn their keep on exactly this: neither bug was visible in the diff as written.
