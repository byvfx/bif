## Learnings
- Rust borrow checker: can't iterate `&self.point_clouds` and call `self.add_instance()` in same loop — collect intermediary
- wgpu storage buffers work well for variable-size point data (vs uniform buffer size limits)
- Poisson disk with spatial hash grid is simple and effective — 4x oversample then reject
- Area-weighted CDF + binary search is the standard approach for triangle sampling
