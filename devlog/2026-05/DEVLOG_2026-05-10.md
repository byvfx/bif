## Key Decisions
- Used canonical `MakeVisible`/`MakeInvisible` over hand-rolled ancestor walk. Less code, USD-correct, but un-hides ancestor siblings as a side effect (USD pruning semantics). Acceptable; documented as a UX TODO.
- `SdfLayer::Find` over raw string compare for root-layer identity. Robust across UNC/case/`file://` mismatches — important on SMB-mounted assets.
- Invisible meshes now allocate CPU vertex arrays (memory cost). Acceptable for correctness; defer-GPU-upload optimization belongs in v0.18 viewport perf milestone.

## Problems and Solutions

## Learnings
- USD `visibility` is pruning, not blocking. Authoring `inherited` doesn't override an ancestor `invisible`. `MakeVisible` exists for exactly this case.
- `cache_prim_data`'s flag guard + LoadNone interaction = silent empty hierarchy. Worth a `// NOT THREAD-SAFE` annotation on the cache mutator; v0.17 task.
- `UsdStage::MuteLayer` on root is a soft TF_CODING_ERROR, not an exception — every caller must gate explicitly.
