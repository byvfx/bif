### Design Decisions

1. **Phase 3 modules use free functions** - `texture_loader`, `ivar_renderer`, and `frustum_culling` use free functions with explicit parameters instead of methods. More testable and clearer dependencies.

2. **`Vertex` in `gpu_types.rs`** - Resolved the open question: Vertex lives in gpu_types since it's fundamentally a GPU layout type, even though mesh_data uses it.

3. **Kept tightly-coupled methods in Renderer** - Functions like `build_ivar_scene`, `start_ivar_render`, `poll_ivar_messages` access many Renderer fields; extracting them would require 10+ params. Left as methods.
