## Learnings

- `Mutex<T>: Sync` requires `T: Send` (not `T: Sync`), so `Arc<Mutex<UsdStage>>` is `Send + Sync` even though UsdStage is only `Send`
- MutexGuard lifetime in `if let Some(ref x)` patterns extends to end of block, causing borrow conflicts with `&mut self` — fix with early extraction or separate blocks
- `Option<MutexGuard<T>>.as_deref()` works cleanly since MutexGuard implements Deref — gives `Option<&T>`
