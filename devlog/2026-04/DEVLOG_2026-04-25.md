## Learnings
- The cleanest workspace-payload integration was to thread policy through the existing stage reload path rather than reviving the stale `PayloadPolicyChanged` app event.
- Sharing a single camera-choice helper between the breadcrumb combo and the `Look Through…` menu keeps the stage camera list from drifting across Qt surfaces.
- Persisted workspace-name migration matters; otherwise old `"render"` settings silently weaken the new Review split.
