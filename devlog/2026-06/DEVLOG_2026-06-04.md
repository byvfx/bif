## Learnings
- **The release notes were always changelog-driven** (ci.yml extracts the matching
  section) — but the *playbook* wasn't documented anywhere live, so the ship steps
  had to be reconstructed. Now captured in CLAUDE.md.
- **Dated changelog headers broke version-specific note extraction** — `\s*\n`
  after `]` can't span ` - DATE`. The fallback to `[Unreleased]` masked it until
  full-prep emptied that section.
- Annotated tags: `git rev-parse --short vX` returns the *tag object* SHA, not the
  commit — deref with `vX^{commit}` to confirm what it points at.
- Challenge suspicious version numbers before tagging — a public release is
  outward-facing and a 5-minor jump (`v1.21.1`) was clearly a typo.
