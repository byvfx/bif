## Key Decisions
- **`rebase --onto` over plain `rebase`** — once the tail is squashed into `main`, the original tail commits aren't recognized by patch-id (the squash combined+changed them), so a plain rebase replays and conflicts. `--onto main <tail-tip>` excludes them by range.
- **Tag at the pre-#5 commit.** v0.16.8 = the dogfood tail only; PathTracer is post-v0.16.8 work → `[Unreleased]`, not `[0.16.8]`.
- **Skills: live commands only.** The `agents/` mirror is deprecated on `main`; re-seeding it with new files made no sense. Kept the working `.claude/commands/` skills.
- **Full MILESTONES reconcile** (vs the handoff's literal minimal ask) — the Released table had silently stopped at v0.16.2; closing the .3/.5/.6 gaps too makes it actually match the tags.

## Learnings
- `git branch -d` refuses a branch that's merged to HEAD but **ahead of its own upstream** (`finish-qt-ui` was 2 ahead of `origin/finish-qt-ui`); needs `-D`. A failing branch in a multi-arg `-d` also aborts the rest of the command.
- The gate needs **both** `setup_usd_env.ps1` and `setup_qt_env.ps1` sourced — USD env alone leaves cxx-qt failing with `QtMissing`. Worth folding into one wrapper.
